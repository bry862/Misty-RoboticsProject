# Overview.
We will be developing a robotic companion pet that reacts to human presence and responds with pet-inspired reactions. For this project, we will be using the Misty platform, developed by Misty Robotics. Our project will rely on Misty's built in sensors to detect humans and perceive the environment (How close it is to a person, are there other people around, are there obstacles between itself and the person). When Misty’s camera sensor detects a person, Misty will perform one of many predetermined tasks, designed to create an interactive experience. 

# The Problem and Opportunity.
Owning a pet can provide companionship. But owning a pet comes with responsibilities not many people can take on or would like to commit to. This is why we want to develop our Misty companion. A robotic pet that allows us to explore the possibility of recreating  the experience of companionship that comes from owning a real pet, but without the commitment or responsibility. We are not trying to make a robot that replaces a real pet. What we will be doing instead is work towards how a robot can analyze its environment and respond to people in a way that feels engaging and companion-like. We will use environmental information, such as objects and the people( as well as their body language) that are around Misty, using the robots sensors. 


# Intended Audience.
We intend our robot to be used at Hunter, with the primary stakeholders being Hunter students and staff. Secondary stakeholders will be thos ein the environment of the primary stakeholders, that will not be interacting with Misty and might still be affected by its movements and or sounds. Interactions are meant to be brief, a person may approach Misty, the sensors detect that person and a response will be triggered based on current conditions. 

The setting gives us an opportunity to integrate Human Centered Design, as we need to be very mindful of our surroundings and mindful of peoples personal space. We need to aim our robots' responses to be non-disruptive, non-intrusive, and safe.
